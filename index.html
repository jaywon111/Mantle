The Spark

Ethereum succeeded beyond imagination, but with success came congestion. Fees soared, throughput stalled, and the promise of global, accessible finance felt out of reach. Something had to change.

In 2022, a group of builders started asking a new question: "What if scaling didn't require trade-offs?" That question became Mantle!

Ethereum L1 ⛓️: ~15 TPS · Mantle's answer? Modular Architecture!

The Vision

Mantle wasn't built to be another rollup. It was designed as a modular L2 — separating execution, data availability, and settlement. This unlocks true scalability without sacrificing security.

Execution

Transactions processed on Mantle's high‑performance rollup.

Settlement

Finalized on Ethereum L1, inherits its security.

Data Availability

Powered by EigenLayer DA for ultra‑low costs.

Modular design 📐 · 4,000+ TPS potential · Near‑zero gas fees

The Bridge

In July 2023, the Mantle Bridge went live. Overnight, users could move ETH, USDC, and other assets between Ethereum L1 and Mantle L2 - trustlessly, efficiently, with finality in minutes instead of days! Amazing right?

Within months, over $100M in TVL crossed the bridge. Value found a new home. Liquidity flowed. The modular thesis was validated.

$100M+ bridged 🌉 · 3–5 min finality · fully audited

The Ecosystem

DeFi, gaming, identity, RWAs: Mantle became the fertile ground where builders plant flags. mETH (Mantle Staked Ether) became one of the largest liquid staking tokens on L2.

Dozens of protocols now live: DEXs, lending markets, perpetuals, and the first L2-native NFT infrastructure.
